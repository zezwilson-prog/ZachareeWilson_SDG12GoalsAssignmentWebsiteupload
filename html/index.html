What is SDG12 ? ?

SDG 12 also known as the Sustainable Development Goal 12 : Responsible Consumption and Production is one of the United Nation’s 17 Sustainable Development Goals which was created in 2015 to ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns by reducing waste, conserving natural resources, and protecting the environment.

What Are SDG 12 Goals ?

1: Reduce waste: Encourage recycling, reusing materials, and reducing food waste. 2: Use resources responsibly: Avoid wasting water, energy, raw materials, and other natural resources. 3: Promote sustainable businesses: Encourage companies to use environmentally friendly production methods. 4: Reduce pollution: Minimize the release of harmful chemicals and waste into the environment. 5: Encourage informed choices: Help people choose products that are durable, reusable, and environmentally friendly.
