SDG 12 PROBLEMS

SDG 12 is trying to address problems caused by wasting resources, producing too much waste, and consuming goods in ways that harm the environment.

7 MAJOR PROBLEMS SDG 12 ARE TRYING TO SOLVE

1. Excessive waste:People throw away large amounts of rubbish, including plastic, paper, clothing, and electronics, which can pollute the environment. 2. Food waste:Large quantities of food are lost or thrown away while many people experience hunger and food insecurity. 3. Plastic pollution:Single-use plastics and other improperly discarded materials pollute rivers, oceans, and land, harming wildlife. 4. Overuse of natural resources:Excessive use of trees, minerals, water, and fossil fuels depletes resources and damages ecosystems. 5. Pollution from production:Factories and other industries can release harmful chemicals, wastewater, and air pollutants during the production of goods. 6. Poor recycling and reuse:Valuable materials are thrown away instead of being repaired, reused, or recycled, increasing the need for new raw materials. 7. Overconsumption:People buying more products than they need can increase demand for manufacturing, packaging, transportation, and natural resources.

Statistics

  • About 1.05 billion tonnes of food were wasted in 2022. Households caused about 60% of it (UNEP, 2024).
  • The world made 62 million tonnes of e-waste in 2022 and only about 22% was recycled (Balde et al., 2024).
  • Food in landfills gives off methane, a strong greenhouse gas.
