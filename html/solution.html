The Solution

The solution to SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production) is to reduce waste, reuse and recycle materials, conserve natural resources, and encourage sustainable ways of producing and consuming goods.

The Three R's

These are the words we should live by if we want a better future .

  • Reduce: Use fewer resources and avoid creating unnecessary waste.
  • Reuse: Use products again, repair them, or pass them on to others.
  • Recycle: Turn suitable waste materials into new products instead of throwing them away.
